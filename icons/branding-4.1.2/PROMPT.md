## Prompt

Create ONE finished production app icon for NEW RENT STORE, a premium Korean rental/property management app. Single square 1024x1024 image, edge-to-edge opaque background (no outside frame, no rounded-square tile, no mockup, no label, no text). Brand direction based on owner's design charter: restrained premium utility, Pure Graphite, precise minimal line symbol, calm professional architecture, compatible with Pretendard typography. Use uniform matte graphite #171B20 background, perfectly clean without texture or vignettes. Center a distinctive architectural monogram combining an uppercase N with two elegant building/doorway outlines: two vertical portal forms of unequal height joined by a single carefully balanced diagonal, with two tiny negative-space doorway/window divisions at most. Strong single coherent silhouette, softly chamfered joins, precise polished vector-like edges. Off-white #EEF0F2 main strokes, ONE muted cobalt #3268C8 small inset architectural face as subtle brand accent; 90 percent monochrome. Symbol entirely inside central 520x520 area, centered optically, occupying about half canvas, ample blank background for circular and squircle Android adaptive masks. Stroke width about 32px at 1024, visible at 48px. Sophisticated architectural identity, no generic emoji house, no roof triangle, no grid of windows, no clip art, no keys or coins, no lettering below, no glow, no 3D bevels, no gradients, no metallic highlights, no shadows, no border. Make the N/building mark feel unique and immediately legible as an understated commercial app brand.
